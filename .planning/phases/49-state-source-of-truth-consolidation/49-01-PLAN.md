<objective>
Make `teams` a derived projection of `players` on the client. Today 14 `setLobby`
handlers spread-update the lobby, three of them leaving `teams` stale or wrong:
`session:host_changed` and `session:avatar_selected` never touch `teams`, and
`session:team_changed` has a push-before-map bug (eventHandlers.ts:152-154) that
pushes the OLD player object — still carrying the old team — into `teams[newTeam]`.

Fix: add a pure `withTeamsDerived(lobby)` helper and wrap it at the SINGLE
`setLobby` site in the Zustand store (useGameState.tsx:128). Every current and
future handler benefits automatically and idempotently.

Purpose: Close the MAINT-04 team-staleness bug class with one authoritative
derivation site instead of per-handler hand-mirroring.
Output: New helper + unit test; one-line store change; three regression tests.
</objective>
