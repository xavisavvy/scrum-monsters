   - Tier 0 (Unranked): all 0%
   - Tier 1 (Novice, +2%): 1% HP, 1% damage
   - Tier 2 (Apprentice, +5%): 2% HP, 2% damage, 1% cooldown, 1% XP, 1% revive
   - Tier 3 (Adept, +8%): 3% HP, 3% damage, 2% cooldown, 2% XP, 2% revive
   - Tier 4 (Expert, +12%): 4% HP, 4% damage, 3% cooldown, 3% XP, 3% revive
   - Tier 5 (Master, +15%): 5% HP, 5% damage, 5% cooldown, 5% XP, 5% revive

   - `MasteryXPAwardedPayload`: `{ lobbyId, playerId, avatarClass, amount, newTotal }`
   - `MasteryTierUpPayload`: `{ lobbyId, playerId, avatarClass, oldTier, newTier, playerName }`

In `server/events/eventTypes.ts`:
