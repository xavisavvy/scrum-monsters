<objective>
Build the two pure helper modules that the MusicControls component (Plan 02) consumes:

1. localStorage history helpers (MUSIC-03)
2. oEmbed title resolution + URL helpers (MUSIC-04), including playlist detection, single-video ID extraction, and playlist ID extraction.

Purpose: These are pure, independently-testable contracts. Building them first means Plan 02 builds the UI against known signatures with zero codebase exploration.
Output: Two new util modules with unit tests.

SCOPE NOTE (team-lead override, supersedes RESEARCH Option A): Playlist support is **accept-only (Option B)**. We do NOT widen the `youtube_play` socket payload, do NOT touch the Zod schema (`shared/socket-schemas.ts`), and do NOT extend the player wrapper with `loadPlaylist`. A `watch?v=...&list=...` URL already plays its first video via the existing `extractVideoId` path. A bare `playlist?list=...` URL is detected, the UI warns it may not play inline, and the raw URL is still submitted. This plan therefore touches only the two new util files — no shared/server/store changes.
</objective>
