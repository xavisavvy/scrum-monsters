<objective>
EXT-02: Promote `AVATAR_CLASSES` (shared/gameEvents.ts:706) from its current shape to a canonical `Record<AvatarClass, ClassDef>` carrying `role`, `baseDamage`, and `icon` for all 10 classes. Derive `HEALER_CLASSES` (CombatManager.ts:171) and `getClassBaseDamage` (CombatManager.ts:607) from it. Retype client `AVATAR_IMAGES` (avatarImages.ts:4) to `Record<AvatarClass, string>` (kept client-side). Collapse the three hand-written `getClassIcon` maps (SpriteRenderer, Lobby, CharacterDetailsPanel) into one registry-backed `icon` lookup — which fixes the missing `monk` icon by construction (the PR #167 monk symptom, subsumed at the root). Acceptance: adding a new `AvatarClass` without a registry entry is a `tsc` error.

Purpose: Per-class data is currently scattered across a shared object, a server switch, a server constant, and three duplicated client maps — adding a class means error-prone shotgun surgery. One typed registry makes it a compile-checked single-file change.
Output: `ClassDef` type, extended `AVATAR_CLASSES`, derived `HEALER_CLASSES`/`getClassBaseDamage`, retyped `AVATAR_IMAGES`, registry-backed icons, and tests.
</objective>
